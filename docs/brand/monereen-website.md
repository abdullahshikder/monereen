# Monereen website archive

**Direction date:** August 2026
**Status:** Active working brief

## Brand identity

Monereen is an artisanal fashion label founded through passion and devoted to personal expression through bespoke creation. What began as a personal project has grown into a deliberate long-term roadmap: establishing a distinct identity, moving through a considered pause, and entering a pivotal phase of scale and impact.

The brand exists to carry heritage and ethical craftsmanship forward through distinct, high-quality work, thoughtful design, rigorous attention to detail, and commitment to the craft.

## Horizon

Build a mature and diversified portfolio across apparel, artisanal craft, and design innovation, rooted in traditional craftsmanship and indigenous textiles close to home.

Long-term milestones:

1. Organic adoption and strong loyalty among primary advocates and close circles.
2. Expanded reach through word of mouth and community engagement.
3. A comprehensive portfolio across multiple product categories.
4. A sustainable supply chain that empowers local artisan communities and supports lasting livelihoods.
5. A growing international presence and global customer base.
6. A flagship retail storefront as the brand’s experiential anchor.
7. A platform celebrating cultural heritage, creativity, and conscious design.

## Website role

The website is a core channel of brand expression. It serves as both a catalogue and presentation of Monereen, alongside a smooth shopping experience.

## Information architecture

### Header

- Left: Monereen logo, Collections, Clothing, Craft, Innovation / Collaborate, About.
- Centre: search and visual discovery across categories, category pages, and product detail pages.
- Right: cart and customer profile.

### Footer

- Left: Monereen logo and brand statement.
- Information: FAQs, policies, journal.
- Connect: contact and Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram, and LinkedIn profiles.

## Category map

- Collections: The Silk Route, The Jamdani Series, Summer ’26, Bridal.
- Clothing: Kaftans, Co-ords, Sets.
- Craft:
  - Bags: Potlis, Totes.
  - Accessories: Scarves.
